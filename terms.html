4. Health and Fitness Disclaimer

4.1 Not medical advice

Luris provides fitness information for general wellbeing and motivation. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, monitor or prevent any disease or condition, and nothing in it, including the weekly plan, workouts, goals, streaks and badges, is medical, nutritional or professional coaching advice.

4.2 Talk to a professional first

Consult a doctor or another qualified health professional before you start any exercise programme or change how active you are, particularly if you have a medical condition or an injury, are pregnant or have recently given birth, take medication, or have not exercised for a while. Stop straight away and seek medical help if you feel pain, chest discomfort, dizziness, faintness or shortness of breath.

4.3 Plans and workouts are general

The weekly plan is generated automatically from the answers you give in the App, such as your goal and the days you want to train, and its workouts come from a general exercise library. They do not take your health, fitness level or medical history into account, and they may not suit you. Choose exercises, weights and an intensity that are safe for you, learn the correct technique, and skip anything you are unsure about.

4.4 You exercise at your own risk

You decide whether, how and how much to exercise. To the extent the law allows, you do so at your own risk. This does not limit the liability that section 15 says cannot be limited.

4.5 The numbers are estimates

Steps, distance, floors, energy, exercise minutes, sleep and body measurements come from Apple Health, which receives them from your iPhone, your Apple Watch or other apps and devices. Luris shows what Apple Health provides and cannot check that it is correct. During walks and runs, distance and pace are measured with GPS, whose accuracy depends on your surroundings, your view of the sky and your device. Calories burned during a workout are estimated from the activity, its duration and the body weight in your profile, and where Apple Health has no active energy or exercise minutes, for example without an Apple Watch, Luris estimates them from your steps, floors and weight. None of these figures is exact, and none of them should be used for medical purposes.

5. Staying Safe on Walks and Runs

Luris records walks and runs with your location. It is not a navigation or emergency service. When you use it:

  • Stay aware of your surroundings, traffic and other people. Do not look at or use your iPhone while crossing a road or wherever it would not be safe.
  • Follow traffic laws and local rules, and use pavements, paths and crossings.
  • Choose routes, times and conditions that are safe for you, and let someone know where you are going if you exercise alone.
  • Recording continues in the background while a session runs, which uses battery. Keep enough charge to call for help.
  • The maps come from Apple and can be incomplete or out of date. They do not show where it is safe or legal to walk or run.
  • A route can reveal where you live or where you often go. Share cards hide the start and end of a route by default, so think before you choose to show them.

You are responsible for where, when and how you walk or run.
