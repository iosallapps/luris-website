2. What Luris does

Luris is a step and fitness tracker for iPhone. It reads your steps and activity from Apple Health, records walks, runs and bike rides with GPS and a route map, builds a six-week plan from a bundled library of 873 exercises, tracks progress with photos and body charts, and offers an optional weekly steps leaderboard on Apple's Game Center.

6. Important: results and your responsibility

Not medical advice. Luris gives fitness information for general wellbeing and motivation. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, monitor or prevent any condition. The weekly plan, workouts, goals, streaks and badges are not medical, nutritional or professional coaching advice. Talk to a doctor before starting an exercise programme, especially with a medical condition or injury, during pregnancy, with medication, or after a long break. Stop and seek medical help if you feel pain, chest discomfort, dizziness, faintness or shortness of breath. You exercise at your own risk, to the extent the law allows.

The numbers are estimates. Steps, distance, floors, energy, exercise minutes, sleep and body measurements come from Apple Health, and Luris cannot check that they are correct. Distance, pace and speed in a walk, run or ride are measured with GPS, whose accuracy depends on your surroundings, your view of the sky and your device. Calories during a workout are estimated, and where Apple Health has no active energy or exercise minutes (for example without an Apple Watch) Luris estimates them from your steps, floors and weight. The weekly plan is generated from your answers and a general exercise library and does not take your medical history into account; skip anything you are unsure about. None of these figures should be used for medical purposes.

Stay safe outdoors. Luris records walks, runs and rides with your location. It is not a navigation or emergency service, and its maps can be incomplete or out of date. Stay aware of traffic and people, follow traffic laws and local rules, and do not hold or look at your iPhone while crossing a road or riding; start, pause and finish a ride only when stopped in a safe place. Recording runs in the background during a session and uses battery. A route can reveal where you live or often go, and share cards hide the start and end of a route by default. You are responsible for where, when and how you walk, run or ride.

The leaderboard. The weekly steps leaderboard is optional, runs on Apple's Game Center, and is for fun: no prizes, and a ranking has no monetary value. If you join, your weekly step total appears under your Game Center alias, which must follow Apple's rules for Game Center and must not be offensive, misleading or impersonate anyone. Do not add steps by hand, use other means to record steps you did not walk, use several accounts or exploit a bug to change your ranking. We may, where Game Center allows it, remove a score or player, report it to Apple or reset or stop the leaderboard. You can leave at any time: Luris then stops sending your score, but Game Center does not let apps delete a posted score, so this week's score stays until the weekly reset.

Your content. Your workouts, routes, progress photos and everything else you create in Luris stay yours. They are stored only on your iPhone, so a device backup is how you protect them. We cannot recover data from a lost, reset or damaged device, or data removed with Delete All Data or Delete Account, which cannot be undone.
