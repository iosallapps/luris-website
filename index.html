Steps and activity

Own your day

A ring for today's steps, with calories, active minutes, distance and floors beside it. The Activity tab turns your week into trends.

  • Steps and distance straight from Apple Health
  • Your own daily step goal
  • Today compared with yesterday

GPS walks and runs

Track every route

Start a walk or a run and Luris draws your route on the map as you go, with live time, distance, pace and calories.

  • Keeps tracking with your iPhone locked
  • A Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
  • Saved to Apple Health as a workout

Weekly plan

Train with a plan

Luris builds a six-week plan sized to how often you train, and lays out every session as a warm-up, the workout and a cool-down.

  • 873 exercises with images and instructions, built into the app
  • Today's session waits on the Home tab
  • Finished workouts saved to Apple Health

Luris is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise programme. Health disclaimer

Progress

Progress you can see

Follow your weight and body fat from Apple Health on clear trend charts, add progress photos, and unlock badges as you go.

  • Progress photos stay on your iPhone
  • 49 badges for steps, distance, workouts, streaks, floors and calories
  • Streaks count the days you reach your step goal

Weekly leaderboard

Climb the ranks

Join the weekly steps leaderboard in Game Center and see where you stand against everyone taking part. It starts over every week.

  • Optional, under your Game Center alias
  • Luris shares one number: your step total for the week
  • Leave at any time

Your health data stays on your iPhone

Luris runs no servers of its own. This is what stays on your iPhone, and exactly what can leave it.

Stays on your iPhone

  • Everything Luris reads from Apple Health
  • Your routes, workouts and progress photos
  • Your profile, plan and settings

Leaves only when you choose, or to Apple

  • Your weekly step total, if you join the leaderboard: to Game Center, under your alias
  • One location, once, so Apple can tell Luris your country and pick kilometres or miles
  • Map requests to Apple Maps while a map is on screen
  • What Sign in with Apple needs, if you use it

Nothing is sent to the developer. No account, no ads, no analytics. Read the full privacy policy and the consumer health data privacy policy.

Questions

More answers, and a direct line to the developer, on the support page.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Luris uses the steps and distance your iPhone records. If you wear an Apple Watch, its steps and activity reach Luris through Apple Health too.

Which iPhones does Luris work on?

Any iPhone with iOS 26.2 or later. There is no iPad or Apple Watch app.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Sign in with Apple is optional and only used to greet you by name.

Can I track bike rides?

No. GPS sessions in Luris are walks and runs.

Which languages does Luris speak?

Sixteen: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Portuguese (Portugal), Romanian, Swedish, Turkish, and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).

How do I delete my data?

Your data is on your iPhone, and the developer holds no copy. The support page shows how to delete it, including workouts Luris saved to Apple Health.
