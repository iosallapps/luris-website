1. Who we are

Visto Apps is the brand of Darius Cirjan, the developer and data controller. This policy explains what the Luris app (iOS) and the website https://lurisapp.com do with personal data.

Contact for any privacy question or request: iosallapps@gmail.com. Website: vistoapps.com. We answer within one month (art. 12 GDPR). We are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer.

Last updated: November 15, 2026.

2. At a glance

  • No online account. Luris has no server of its own that receives your data. Sign in with Apple is optional and only fills in your name.
  • Your health data stays on your iPhone. It is never used for ads or analytics, never sold, and never sent to us or to Google or RevenueCat. The one thing you can choose to send is your weekly step total, to Apple's Game Center, if you join the leaderboard.
  • Workouts, routes, progress photos and settings stay on your iPhone, and Profile, Your data, Delete All Data removes them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Personalised ads only with your consent; in the EEA, UK and Switzerland no ad loads before you answer the consent form. Luris Pro removes ads.
  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) start only once Google's consent regime allows them; they never contain health, activity or location values. Crash reports help us fix bugs.
  • We do not sell personal data for money.

3. Data that stays on your device

Apple Health. With your permission Luris reads from Apple Health: steps, walking and running distance, flights climbed, active energy, exercise minutes, sleep, weight and body fat percentage. It writes to Apple Health the walks, runs, bike rides and workouts you finish, as workouts, with their active energy and their walking and running distance (cycling distance for a ride). It writes no weight and no route. This is data concerning health under art. 9 GDPR. It is processed on your device, on the basis of your explicit consent: the permission you give in the iOS Health sheet, which you can withdraw anytime in Settings, Privacy & Security, Health, Luris. Health data is never used for advertising, marketing or analytics, never sold, never sent to our servers (we have none) and never sent to Google, RevenueCat or any other third party. No Apple Health value appears in an analytics event, a user property, an ad request or a crash report. The one exception is chosen by you: if you join the weekly leaderboard, one number derived from Apple Health, your total steps for the week, is posted to Apple's Game Center under your alias (see the table below).

Everything else stays on the iPhone. Your profile, goals, plan, workouts, walk, run and ride history and routes, habits, challenges, achievements, progress photos, settings and the numbers shown in the widgets are stored in the app's storage on your iPhone. Luris has no account on a server. Sign in with Apple is optional and only fills in your name: your Apple user ID and name are kept on your iPhone, and no token is stored or sent. Notifications are scheduled on the device. Photos you take or pick are kept in the app's storage. A share card leaves your iPhone only when you send it somewhere yourself.

Deleting it. In the app, open Profile, then Your data, and tap Delete All Data. It removes everything Luris keeps on your iPhone: the database, progress photos, leaderboard membership and public name, the Sign in with Apple ID and name, settings, widget data, notifications and any Live Activity. Delete Account, shown when you are signed in with Apple, does the same and signs you out. Two things stay and the confirmation says so: what Luris saved to Apple Health (delete it in the Health app) and scores already posted to Game Center (Game Center manages them). Deleting the app removes the app's data too, but the Sign in with Apple details can survive it, so use Delete All Data first.

5. Permissions

The app asks for these permissions only when you use the feature, and works without them. Change them anytime in your device settings.

  • Apple Health (read and write, per type). You choose in the iOS Health sheet which types Luris may read and which it may write, and can change that anytime in Settings, Privacy & Security, Health, Luris. Without it the app still opens but cannot show your steps.
  • Location (while using the app, also with the screen locked during a session). To record the route, distance, pace and speed of a walk, run or ride. Tracking continues in the background only while you have started a session. Routes stay on your device. If you give only approximate location, Luris can ask for precise location for that one session.
  • Camera. To take a photo for a share card or a progress photo. Photos stay on your device.
  • Photos (add only). To save a share card to your photo library. Luris cannot read your library. When you pick a photo for a card or for progress, you use the system photo picker and Luris receives only the photo you choose.
  • Notifications. Local reminders scheduled on the device. No push server is involved.
  • Tracking (iOS, App Tracking Transparency). Asked once, after the consent form. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised with it.
  • Game Center (optional). Sign-in is handled by iOS. Luris uses it only for the weekly steps leaderboard, and only after you join from the leaderboard screen.

6. Consent, ads and tracking

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app first shows Google's consent form (a certified consent management platform under the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework). Until you answer it, no ad is requested and no usage statistics are sent. If you refuse personalised ads, you still get limited, non-personalised ads.

On iOS the App Tracking Transparency prompt comes after the consent form. Ads load only once it is answered.

Change your choice anytime: in the app, Profile, About, Privacy Choices (shown where the consent form applies). On iOS, also Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Ad personalisation signals stay off in Google Analytics whatever you answer; AdMob requests are limited (non-personalised) unless the consent form or your region allows personalised ads.

Luris Pro and trial users see no ads and are not asked for ad consent.

Buying the paid version removes ads.

7. Purchases

Purchases are made through App Store. The store processes the payment; we receive only a pseudonymous receipt, checked through RevenueCat, that tells the app which purchase you own. We never see your card or account details.

8. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Data held by the services above is kept for the period listed in the table. We do not keep copies of our own. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to answer, and at most 2 years.

9. Transfers outside the EEA

Some providers (Google, RevenueCat and others marked in the table) process data in the United States or other countries. Transfers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified companies or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (art. 45 and 46 GDPR).

10. Your rights (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing (including to object at any time to processing based on legitimate interest), to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.

Because the app has no account, we usually cannot tell which data at Google or RevenueCat is yours. Write to iosallapps@gmail.com and include, if you can, the RevenueCat ID shown in the app or your purchase receipt, and we will forward the request to the provider. You can always delete all data on the device by deleting the app.

You can complain to the supervisory authority where you live or work, or to ours: Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal (ANSPDCP), www.dataprotection.ro. The list of EU authorities: edpb.europa.eu.

11. United States

  • We do not sell personal information for money.
  • When you allow personalised ads, Google AdMob uses your advertising identifier for ads based on activity in other apps. Some US state laws (for example California) call this "sharing" or "targeted advertising". You can opt out at any time: refuse tracking on iOS (Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking), or email us and we will treat it as an opt-out request.
  • Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws can ask to know, correct or delete their personal information, and will not be treated differently for doing so. Categories collected in the last 12 months are the ones in the table above (identifiers, internet activity, approximate location from IP, purchase records). We do not collect health data: it stays on your device, and the only health-derived value you can choose to send is your weekly step total to Apple's Game Center. Requests: iosallapps@gmail.com.

12. Children

Luris is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in their country (13 to 16 in the EU). If you believe a child gave us data, write to iosallapps@gmail.com and we will delete what we can reach. Ads in the app are not designed for children.

13. Security

All network connections use HTTPS. Data on the device is protected by the operating system's app sandbox and encryption. No system is perfectly secure, so we keep what leaves your device to the minimum listed here.

14. This website

lurisapp.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which records the IP address of visitors in server logs for security. The site sets no cookies, runs no scripts, uses no analytics or ads and loads no third-party resources, including its fonts. The App Store links open Apple's site, which has its own privacy policy.

15. Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, we update this page and the date at the top before the new version ships. Important changes are also mentioned in the store update notes.

16. Consumer health data

This section is the consumer health data privacy policy for Luris, for Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar laws in other US states. It adds to the sections above and changes none of them.

  • What we collect: nothing. We, the developer, do not collect, receive or store consumer health data. The health and fitness data Luris uses comes from Apple Health, with your permission, and from what you enter in the app (see Data that stays on your device). It is processed only on your iPhone.
  • Why it is used: only to show you your activity, trends, plan, progress and achievements in the app.
  • Sharing: we do not share or sell consumer health data, and no affiliate or other third party receives any from us. Google's ad, analytics and crash services never receive it (Consent, ads and tracking). The only value based on it that can leave your iPhone is your weekly step total, and only if you join the leaderboard: Luris then sends it to Apple's Game Center at your direction (Services that receive data).
  • Your rights: you can see and delete this data on your iPhone (Deleting it), withdraw your consent at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > Luris, and stop sharing your step total by leaving the leaderboard. For any other request, email iosallapps@gmail.com. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and we will respond within 45 days.
