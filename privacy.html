Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

The short version. Luris keeps your health and fitness data on your device. We run no servers, no analytics and no advertising, and we have no account system of our own. The only information that ever leaves your iPhone is a single weekly step total, and only if you choose to join the leaderboard.

1. Who we are

Luris ("the app") is an iOS fitness app published by an independent developer. This policy explains what the app does with your information. Contact: iosallapps@gmail.com.

2. Health and fitness data

With your permission, Luris reads the following from Apple Health to show your activity and progress: steps, workouts, active and resting energy, walking, running and cycling distance, flights climbed, exercise and stand time, heart rate and resting heart rate, sleep, and body metrics such as weight, height, body fat and lean mass.

With your permission, Luris also writes back to Apple Health: workouts you complete in the app, the active energy and distance recorded during them, and body weight you log yourself.

This data is read directly from Apple Health on your device and stored only on your device. It is not uploaded, not backed up to our servers, because we have none, and never sold, shared or used for advertising. You can withdraw access at any time in the Health app under Sharing, and you can delete everything Luris stored by deleting the app.

3. Location

When you start a run or walk, Luris uses your location while the app is in use to draw your route and measure distance and pace. Routes are stored on your device as part of the workout. They are not sent anywhere. Location is only requested when you begin tracking a workout.

4. Camera and photos

Luris asks for camera access so you can take progress photos and a profile picture, and for permission to save images you export from the app to your photo library. Progress photos and your avatar stay on your device. Luris does not read your photo library.

5. Sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional and uses Apple's own service. Luris receives an anonymous user identifier and, if you allow it, your name, which is used only to greet you inside the app. We never see your Apple password, and Apple's Hide My Email option is fully supported.

6. The leaderboard

The weekly steps leaderboard is opt in. If you join it, Luris submits one number to Apple's Game Center: your total step count for the current week. Your ranking name is the Game Center nickname you chose with Apple, not something Luris supplies.

Nothing else is submitted. No per-day breakdown, no workouts, no heart rate, no sleep, no routes, no location. If you never join, nothing is sent at all.

7. What we do not do

Analytics or crash SDKsNone. The app contains no third-party tracking libraries.
AdvertisingNone. Your health data is never used for advertising or marketing.
Selling dataNever. We do not sell, rent or trade any information.
Tracking across appsNone. Luris does not track you across other apps or websites.
Accounts on our serversWe operate no servers and hold no database of users.

8. The one outbound request

Some exercise illustrations are downloaded on demand from a public, read-only open-source image repository hosted on GitHub. These requests contain no personal data and no health data. As with any web request, the host can see your IP address. All other content ships inside the app.

9. Device backup

If you use iCloud Backup or encrypted local backups, Apple includes the app's on-device data in that backup, the same as for any other app. That backup is controlled by Apple and by you, under Apple's privacy terms. Luris itself does not sync your health data to iCloud.

10. Children

Luris is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Because the app collects nothing centrally, there is nothing for us to delete on request, but a parent or guardian can remove all local data by deleting the app.

11. Your rights

Because your data never leaves your device, you keep direct control of it. You can revoke Health, location, camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings at any time, leave the leaderboard from inside the app, and erase everything by deleting Luris. Under the GDPR, our legal basis for processing health data is your explicit consent, given through the iOS permission prompts and withdrawable at any time.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will be announced in the app.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request about your data: iosallapps@gmail.com.
